02 · Strategy

How every campaign gets built

A consistent framework applied to every ad account — from first audience test to weekly reporting.

01

Audience targeting & segmentation

Building and layering cold, warm, lookalike and retargeting audiences so budget reaches the people most likely to convert.

02

Creative strategy & A/B testing

Working with designers and editors to test hooks, formats and CTAs, then scaling only what the data says is working.

03

Budget & bid management

Daily monitoring and reallocation across ad sets so spend follows performance, not the original plan.

04

Conversion rate optimization

Pairing ad delivery with landing page and funnel improvements to lift lead quality, not just lead volume.

05

Meta & TikTok pixel setup

Clean tracking foundations — pixels, events and attribution — so every optimization decision rests on accurate data.

06

Reporting & insights

Clear, client-facing performance reports that translate CPL, CTR and ROAS into decisions the business can act on.
